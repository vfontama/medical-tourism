Safety notes

CareBridge helps compare options, but it does not provide medical advice. Patients should verify credentials, request a written treatment estimate, confirm visa requirements, and speak with a licensed clinician before traveling.

Ratings and cost bands are feedback-style signals for comparison, not clinical quality guarantees or exact prices.
